# Market Comparison Matrix

- **Date:** 2026-07-21
- **Scope:** Ten completed detailed-review subjects, compared against Candidate A (experienced chief organizers of larger or more complex martial-arts seminars) and Candidate B (first-time or infrequent martial-arts seminar organizers).
- **Decision boundary:** This matrix summarizes existing evidence. It does not select a segment, establish demand, authorize a prototype, or authorize development. See `docs/market-research/MARKET_RESEARCH_PLAN.md` and `docs/validation/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS_TEMPLATE.md`.

## A. Status and interpretation rules

- `Yes`, `Partial`, `No`, and `Unknown` retain the meanings used by the detailed profiles. `Unknown` means the inspected evidence is insufficient; it is not evidence that the capability is absent.
- Ratings summarize documented overlap, not usability, effectiveness, quality, or customer satisfaction.
- Product features, informational guidance, and composite workflows are different kinds of evidence and must not be treated as equivalent.
- No authenticated product, trial, paid plan, registration, or payment flow was tested. The only bounded public-product observation already recorded was of Kihapp public event and demonstration pages; it did not include data changes or an authenticated organizer workflow. See `docs/market-research/competitors/KIHAPP_PROFILE.md`.
- Candidate relevance is a research judgment, not evidence of adoption, payment, or product-market fit. See `docs/market-research/MARKET_RESEARCH_PLAN.md`.
- Prices, currencies, plan boundaries, regions, and product access can change after the source checks dated 2026-07-19 and 2026-07-20. The controlling profile must be consulted before reusing a pricing claim.

## C. Candidate A comparison

### Strongest substitute or overlap

1. **Organizer memory plus reusable personal templates** is the strongest incumbent substitute because it is embedded in the experienced organizer's method, preserves private judgment, and can absorb any supporting tool. Its weakness is transferability, not lack of flexibility. See `docs/market-research/competitors/ORGANIZER_MEMORY_PLUS_REUSABLE_PERSONAL_TEMPLATES_PROFILE.md` and `docs/validation/interviews/INT_A3_DECISION.md`.
2. **Messaging plus Google Drive** is the strongest tool workflow because it provides familiar communication, flexible private/shared files, versions, offline access, and low incremental cost; one mature team reported that it worked well. See `docs/market-research/competitors/MESSAGING_PLUS_GOOGLE_DRIVE_PROFILE.md` and `docs/validation/interviews/INT_A4_DECISION.md`.
3. **Planning Pod** is the broadest verified general event-operations product: reusable plans, owners, deadlines, permissions, registration, files, travel/lodging data, and post-event learning overlap much of Candidate A. See `docs/market-research/competitors/PLANNING_POD_PROFILE.md`.
4. **PlanBrite** strongly overlaps complex professional event planning through workbacks, linked production records, risks, approvals, and run-of-show management. Its professional-production model and unverified martial-arts fit keep it below the incumbent workflows. See `docs/market-research/competitors/PLANBRITE_PROFILE.md`.
5. **Asana** is the strongest generic task/dependency baseline, but adaptation, paid feature gates, accounts, and lack of seminar-specific reasoning create a higher switching threshold than a familiar personal method. See `docs/market-research/competitors/ASANA_EVENT_PLANNING_TEMPLATE_PROFILE.md`.

### Meaningful but partial overlap

- **Gymdesk** covers real martial-arts seminar enrollment and execution, yet not the long-range private preparation model. See `docs/market-research/competitors/GYMDESK_PROFILE.md`.
- **Eventbrite** covers attendee, promotion, finance, reporting, and check-in jobs, while internal preparation remains elsewhere. See `docs/market-research/competitors/EVENTBRITE_PROFILE.md`.
- **SignUpGenius** covers helper/attendee execution with low participant friction, but not Candidate A's complete organizer job. See `docs/market-research/competitors/SIGNUPGENIUS_PROFILE.md`.

### Limited relevance to Candidate A's full job

- **Kihapp** is relevant when a seminar includes registration or competition operations, but its verified core remains tournament execution rather than organizer preparation. See `docs/market-research/competitors/KIHAPP_PROFILE.md`.
- **An Event Planning Checklist for Any Event** is a useful audit source, not a private or operational system. See `docs/market-research/competitors/EVENTBRITE_EVENT_PLANNING_CHECKLIST_PROFILE.md`.

This grouping does not select Candidate A. INT-A3 supplies strong participant-level problem evidence, while INT-A4 and the incumbent workflows raise a high routine-adoption and switching threshold. See `docs/validation/interviews/INT_A3_DECISION.md` and `docs/validation/interviews/INT_A4_DECISION.md`.

## D. Candidate B comparison

### Strongest substitute or overlap

- **Guidance:** `An Event Planning Checklist for Any Event` is the strongest informational alternative because it is free, ungated, broad, and explanatory. It is general rather than martial-arts-specific and has no operational state. See `docs/market-research/competitors/EVENTBRITE_EVENT_PLANNING_CHECKLIST_PROFILE.md`.
- **Reusable operational planning:** Asana is the strongest generic task-template baseline; its free tier and official event template reduce blank-page effort, while several useful controls are paid and helpers normally need accounts. See `docs/market-research/competitors/ASANA_EVENT_PLANNING_TEMPLATE_PROFILE.md`.
- **Low-friction helpers and participants:** SignUpGenius provides no-account slot claiming, reminders, reusable templates, and a separate ticketing/check-in workflow. It does not explain the full seminar. See `docs/market-research/competitors/SIGNUPGENIUS_PROFILE.md`.
- **Attendee-facing execution:** Eventbrite strongly covers publishing, registration, ticketing, promotion, reminders, reporting, and check-in, including free publishing/free tickets, but Bulgarian paid-event support is unresolved. See `docs/market-research/competitors/EVENTBRITE_PROFILE.md`.
- **Familiar low-cost host workflow:** Messaging plus Google Drive can host any downloaded or personal playbook and keeps helper coordination familiar, but Candidate B must still obtain and structure the guidance. See `docs/market-research/competitors/MESSAGING_PLUS_GOOGLE_DRIVE_PROFILE.md`.

### Meaningful but partial overlap

- **PlanBrite** generates connected professional event documents but may be too producer-oriented and costly for an infrequent organizer. See `docs/market-research/competitors/PLANBRITE_PROFILE.md`.
- **Planning Pod** can operate a first event but is broad, paid, and setup-heavy relative to a lightweight guided path. See `docs/market-research/competitors/PLANNING_POD_PROFILE.md`.
- **Kihapp** and **Gymdesk** reduce martial-arts event administration but teach product configuration or tournament operations rather than a complete first-seminar method. See `docs/market-research/competitors/KIHAPP_PROFILE.md` and `docs/market-research/competitors/GYMDESK_PROFILE.md`.
- **Organizer memory plus reusable personal templates** can help only if the novice receives usable artifacts and enough explanation or mentoring. See `docs/market-research/competitors/ORGANIZER_MEMORY_PLUS_REUSABLE_PERSONAL_TEMPLATES_PROFILE.md`.

### Limited relevance

None of the ten shortlisted subjects has negligible Candidate B relevance; the shortlist was intentionally decision-critical. However, **none provides evidence of actual first-time-organizer use in the target segment**. Candidate B has not been directly interviewed or observed. See `docs/validation/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS_TEMPLATE.md` and `docs/validation/interviews/INT_A4_DECISION.md`.

Operational breadth, low-friction access, and guidance must remain separate: an article can explain without executing; Eventbrite/SignUpGenius can execute without a full playbook; Drive/chat can be easy to access without supplying seminar knowledge. No segment decision follows from these relevance ratings alone.

## E. Market coverage summary

### Jobs already strongly covered

- Generic reusable tasks, owners, deadlines, completion, dependencies, files, and status are strongly covered by Asana and Planning Pod; PlanBrite adds connected professional production planning. See `docs/market-research/competitors/ASANA_EVENT_PLANNING_TEMPLATE_PROFILE.md`, `docs/market-research/competitors/PLANNING_POD_PROFILE.md`, and `docs/market-research/competitors/PLANBRITE_PROFILE.md`.
- Registration, tickets, payments, capacity, attendance/check-in, reminders, and reporting are strongly covered in different combinations by Eventbrite, SignUpGenius, Gymdesk, and Kihapp. Regional and use-case boundaries still apply. See their controlling profiles.
- Promotion execution is strongly covered by Eventbrite, while broad promotion guidance is free in the Eventbrite checklist. See `docs/market-research/competitors/EVENTBRITE_PROFILE.md` and `docs/market-research/competitors/EVENTBRITE_EVENT_PLANNING_CHECKLIST_PROFILE.md`.
- Familiar chat, calls, cloud files, versions, permissions, and manually reusable artifacts are already available through messaging/Drive and organizer-owned templates. See `docs/market-research/competitors/MESSAGING_PLUS_GOOGLE_DRIVE_PROFILE.md` and `docs/market-research/competitors/ORGANIZER_MEMORY_PLUS_REUSABLE_PERSONAL_TEMPLATES_PROFILE.md`.

### Jobs partially covered

- Private organizer information, contingencies, instructor travel/accommodation, merchandise preparation, and post-event learning can be represented in several products or incumbent workflows, but coverage is usually generic, manual, incomplete, or plan-dependent. See the B2 controlling profiles.
- Lightweight confirmation exists in SignUpGenius participation, chat reactions, and some task products, but no evidence connects an equally low-friction helper action to a private seminar-specific organizer plan. See `docs/market-research/competitors/SIGNUPGENIUS_PROFILE.md` and `docs/market-research/competitors/MESSAGING_PLUS_GOOGLE_DRIVE_PROFILE.md`.

### Jobs still weak or `Unknown`

- Martial-arts seminar-specific `what and why` guidance for an actual first-time organizer.
- Structured guest-instructor invitation, travel, accommodation, pickup, preferences, and teaching logistics tied to a preparation timeline.
- Context-aware, restricted contingency reasoning that avoids prompting obvious local risks.
- Transfer of selected tacit rationale, priorities, dependencies, and contingency triggers from an expert to a novice or successor.
- Evidence that a target organizer will adopt, repeatedly use, or pay for a new workflow instead of a checklist, personal template, mentor, chat/Drive, or an adjacent product. See `docs/validation/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS_TEMPLATE.md` and the ten controlling profiles.

### Strongest reviewed alternatives

- **Strongest overall product competitor:** Planning Pod for broad event operations; Asana remains the strongest generic task/dependency baseline. See `docs/market-research/competitors/PLANNING_POD_PROFILE.md` and `docs/market-research/competitors/ASANA_EVENT_PLANNING_TEMPLATE_PROFILE.md`.
- **Strongest informational substitute:** `An Event Planning Checklist for Any Event`. See `docs/market-research/competitors/EVENTBRITE_EVENT_PLANNING_CHECKLIST_PROFILE.md`.
- **Strongest tool workflow:** Messaging plus Google Drive. See `docs/market-research/competitors/MESSAGING_PLUS_GOOGLE_DRIVE_PROFILE.md`.
- **Strongest experience-based substitute:** Organizer memory plus reusable personal templates. See `docs/market-research/competitors/ORGANIZER_MEMORY_PLUS_REUSABLE_PERSONAL_TEMPLATES_PROFILE.md`.
