## 6. Candidate A assessment

### Supporting evidence

- **[OD]** Claimable role/task slots, recurring dates/times, quantities, reminders, response changes, access controls, and additional administrators cover volunteer/helper execution. See SG-S3 through SG-S10.
- **[OS]** The separate Events product covers ticket types, attendee questions, capacity, confirmations, reminders, QR tickets, dashboard tracking, and exports. See SG-S16.
- **[OD]** Payments, reports, item variations, and ticket scanning cover several operational and event-day jobs. See SG-S11, SG-S14, SG-S15, SG-S17, and SG-S21.

### Contradicting or limiting evidence

- **[OD]** The documented “tasks” are claimable participant slots, not an organizer project plan with arbitrary owners, subtasks, deadlines, and dependencies. See SG-S3 and SG-S4.
- **[RI]** No verified private contingency cockpit, instructor travel/accommodation workflow, critical-path handling, or structured seminar knowledge transfer matches Candidate A's deeper hypothesis. See SG-R2 and SG-R3.
- **[OP]** Waitlists/admins/custom questions/privacy/customization are plan-limited, and current payment-account setup is not verified for Bulgaria. See SG-S2, SG-S9, SG-S10, SG-S13, and SG-S14.
- **[OS]** Ticketing and helper logistics do not establish long-range event preparation merely because they are event-related. See SG-S16 and SG-R2.

### Important unknowns

- Whether a mature seminar team would use SignUpGenius alongside current chat/Drive without duplicating data.
- Bulgarian organizer eligibility, currency, bank withdrawal, and exact fee treatment.
- Whether mobile web and the iOS scanner remain dependable with weak connectivity.
- Any undocumented integration that could connect sign-ups to a separate planning system.

### Researcher inference

**[RI]** SignUpGenius is a meaningful execution-layer comparator for Candidate A, especially for helpers, attendees, money, and check-in. It does not currently displace the broader planning/private-control hypothesis, so its relevance is material but not high across the full Candidate A job. See SG-S3 through SG-S17, SG-R2, and SG-R3.

**Final Candidate A relevance: `Medium`.**

## 7. Candidate B assessment

### Supporting evidence

- **[OD]** A staged builder, examples, help comments, editable templates, duplication, recurring dates, and slots reduce blank-page setup. See SG-S3, SG-S4, SG-S12, and SG-S18.
- **[OS]** Helpers/participants can claim slots, register, or pay without creating accounts; confirmations and reminders reduce follow-up. See SG-S1 and SG-S5.
- **[OP]** A permanent `$0.00` plan includes unlimited sign-up pages and core reminders/groups/duplication/payments; current Events ticketing is available on Free. See SG-S2 and SG-S16.
- **[OD]** Access codes, hidden participant details, quantity limits, edits/deletion, waitlists, reports, and calendar/mobile support cover common lightweight administration. See SG-S6 through SG-S11 and SG-S19.

### Contradicting or limiting evidence

- **[OD]** Templates guide the construction of a sign-up page, not all seminar tasks or why they matter. See SG-S18.
- **[RI]** Instructor travel, venue/contract preparation, contingencies, task dependencies, and structured post-event learning are weak or unknown. See SG-R2 and SG-R4.
- **[OP]** Free pages contain ads; custom questions, file features, texts, multiple admins, waitlists, and advanced controls require paid tiers. See SG-S2, SG-S9, SG-S10, and SG-S13.
- **[OD]** General mobile use is browser-based and ticket scanning is iOS-only, which may constrain event-day roles. See SG-S17 and SG-S19.

### Important unknowns

- Whether SignUpGenius templates contain a martial-arts/seminar starting point with sufficient content depth.
- Actual novice setup time, error recovery, and helper comprehension.
- Bulgarian paid-event/payment eligibility and currency.
- Whether current free-plan advertising is acceptable to target organizers/participants.

### Researcher inference

**[RI]** SignUpGenius is a high-relevance Candidate B comparator because it demonstrates low-account-friction coordination, reminders, templates, reuse, and a capable free tier. Its gap is not simple registration or helper confirmation; it is the broader seminar-specific preparation and explanation layer. See SG-S1 through SG-S19, SG-R2, and SG-R4.

**Final Candidate B relevance: `High`.**

## 9. Differentiation implications

### Capabilities already covered

- **[OD]** Volunteer/helper slots, dates/times, quantities, no-account participation, reminders, confirmations, security, limited privacy, additional admins, reports, duplication, and editable templates. See SG-S3 through SG-S13 and SG-S18.
- **[OS]** Ticket registration, attendee data, multiple ticket types, capacity/sold-out behavior, QR delivery, reminders, tracking, and exports. See SG-S16.
- **[OD]** Payments, refunds, merchandise variants, and iOS ticket scanning. See SG-S14, SG-S15, SG-S17, and SG-S21.

### Capabilities partially covered

- **[OD]** Slots approximate task ownership, scheduled controls approximate deadlines, visibility/admin controls approximate private organizer work, and reports approximate post-event records; none establishes a full planning cockpit. See SG-S2 through SG-S13.
- **[OS]** Stores and outreach channels cover merchandise sales and promotion execution, but not procurement/checkpoints or a campaign plan. See SG-S1, SG-S2, and SG-S21.

### Credible remaining gaps

- **[RI]** Long-range seminar preparation with dependencies, critical deadlines, and domain-specific omission prevention. See SG-R2 through SG-R4.
- **[RI]** Structured instructor travel/accommodation and private contingency decisions. See SG-R2 and SG-R3.
- **[RI]** Post-event lessons and knowledge transfer connected back into the next seminar plan. See SG-R2 through SG-R4.

### Unknowns that are not proven gaps

- Bulgarian payments/currency, authenticated usability, mobile/offline reliability, and breadth of third-party integrations.
- Whether a suitable seminar template already exists in the current library.
- Whether participants/helpers and organizers accept ads or paid-tier boundaries in real target events.

### Features that should not be copied merely because SignUpGenius has them

- **[RI]** Fundraising, group gifts, full online-store breadth, ticket scanning, marketing texts, custom portals, and enterprise SSO are adjacent capabilities, not validated target requirements. See SG-S1, SG-S2, SG-R3, and SG-R4.
- **[RI]** The evidence supports learning from low-friction participation, not automatically reproducing SignUpGenius's complete product suite. See SG-R2.

## 10. Classification review

**Classification result: `KEEP AS ADJACENT`.**

**[RI]** SignUpGenius solves concrete seminar-adjacent jobs—helpers, participant registration, ticketing, payments, reminders, communications, and check-in—without establishing the long-range organizer preparation or martial-arts specialization required for `DIRECT`. Its high Candidate B and meaningful Candidate A overlap rule out deprioritization. See SG-S1 through SG-S21, SG-R1, and SG-R2.

**Proposed inventory correction:** **[RI]** Preserve `ADJACENT`, Candidate A `Medium`, and Candidate B `High`, but in a later approved inventory edit expand the primary-job summary beyond volunteer sign-ups: the current product now has a distinct Events workflow with ticket types, attendee questions, capacity, QR delivery/check-in, and all-plan access. Also replace any stale payment-fee note with the current `3.95% + $0.95` public amount and retain the US/Canada organizer-payment boundary. See SG-S2 and SG-S14 through SG-S17.

## 11. Confidence and evidence gaps

## 12. Profile conclusion

**[RI]** SignUpGenius is a strong adjacent comparator for lightweight helper/participant coordination and now also for basic ticketed events. It verifies that no-account slot claiming, reminders, quantities, editable templates, reuse, capacity, payments, and check-in can be packaged with a permanent free tier, while advanced administration and customization remain paid. It does not establish complete seminar planning, dependencies, private contingencies, instructor logistics, or knowledge transfer, and Bulgarian payments are unverified. The profile preserves `KEEP AS ADJACENT`, Candidate A relevance `Medium`, and Candidate B relevance `High`; it does not select a final segment and does not authorize prototype or development work.
