## 6. Candidate A assessment

### Supporting evidence

- **[OD]** Free/paid tickets, custom attendee questions, per-type/total capacity, waitlists, confirmations, refunds, orders, and exports cover complex attendee administration. See EB-S6 through EB-S9, EB-S14, and EB-S21.
- **[OD]** Organization roles/permissions and the iOS/Android Organizer app support divided event-day responsibilities, sales, and check-in. See EB-S10 and EB-S12.
- **[OS]** Marketplace discovery, email/social advertising, tracking, and analytics cover promotion and performance measurement. See EB-S17 and EB-S20.
- **[OD]** Payouts, finance reports, multi-event analytics, APIs, and event duplication support recurring operational continuity. See EB-S11, EB-S15, EB-S16, and EB-S18.

### Contradicting or limiting evidence

- **[OD]** Team permissions govern Eventbrite functions; they do not establish internal preparation tasks with owners, deadlines, or dependencies. See EB-S10.
- **[RI]** Private contingency planning, structured instructor travel/accommodation, merchandise procurement/checkpoints, and lessons learned are absent from verified capability evidence and therefore remain weak/unknown. See EB-R2 and EB-R3.
- **[OD]** Copying an event omits waitlists, tracking links, attendee emails, online pages, and attendee data, limiting playbook completeness. See EB-S11.
- **[OP]** Bulgaria is not on EPP, Pro, ticket-fee, or at-door tables; paid-event applicability for a Bulgarian organization is unresolved. See EB-S2 through EB-S5.

### Important unknowns

- Whether a Bulgarian bank/organization can run paid Eventbrite events through PayPal and the exact currency/fees.
- Whether organizer roles can be restricted narrowly enough for the target team's sensitive operational data without extra administration.
- Any current native planning/document integration not found in the inspected official sources.
- Actual setup/support burden for a multi-day international seminar and weak-connectivity check-in.

### Researcher inference

**[RI]** Eventbrite is important to Candidate A for attendee, finance, promotion, and event-day execution, but it does not establish the experienced organizer's private long-range preparation system. It is more likely a complementary or partial replacement tool than the complete planning cockpit under study. See EB-S6 through EB-S18, EB-R2, and EB-R3.

**Final Candidate A relevance: `Medium`.**

## 7. Candidate B assessment

- **[OD]** The staged event builder guides page details, date/location/agenda, ticket types, order forms, confirmation, privacy, organizer, and refund setup. See EB-S6.
- **[OP]** Unlimited events and all free tickets can be published without organizer or ticketing fees. See EB-S2 and EB-S3.
- **[OD]** Capacity, waitlists, confirmations, reminders, refunds, attendee management, exports, and check-in reduce several novice execution burdens. See EB-S7 through EB-S14 and EB-S21.
- **[OS]** Built-in marketplace/promotion, email campaigns, organizer profiles/followers, and analytics centralize outreach and feedback signals. See EB-S16, EB-S17, and EB-S20.
