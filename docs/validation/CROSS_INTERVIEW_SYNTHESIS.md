# Cross-Interview Validation Synthesis

## 1. Purpose and decision boundary

This document combines the formal evidence in every completed interview NOTES/DECISION pair currently committed under `docs/validation/interviews/`. It asks:

- which organizational problems recur;
- which participant segments report or observe them;
- how often and how severely they appear in the available records;
- which consequences actually occurred;
- how adequate current alternatives are; and
- whether the evidence supports further research, a paper workflow, a low-fidelity test, a narrow MVP, or no product work.

The source boundary is strict: only the formal NOTES and DECISION files listed in Section 2 are treated as interview evidence. Existing synthesis, market-research, plan, guide, template, raw audio, raw transcript, private source path, email, meeting link, and outside information are not used as evidence for the findings below.

Evidence labels are preserved throughout: **[PR] Participant report** for reported past/current behavior or incidents; **[PO] Participant opinion** for judgments, preferences, predictions, and recommendations; **[CF] Contaminated concept feedback** for responses after concept disclosure or leading framing; **[RI] Researcher inference** for cross-interview interpretation; and **[U] Unknown** for absent, unclear, or unverified evidence. A label describes evidence type, not truth across a population.

This is qualitative, non-statistical research. Seven completed evidence packages, including one pilot, cannot establish market prevalence, product-market fit, adoption, willingness to pay, or causal product impact. Counts below refer to unique interview IDs, not statements. A repeated example from one participant remains one participant perspective. Participant opinions, contaminated concept feedback, encouragement, and offers to review later work are not converted into demand evidence.

The available NOIT regulation is for the 2025-2026 competition year. Its dates, procedures, direction wording, online-availability rules, documentation format, forms, and evaluation details may change and must be verified against the regulation for the actual later year of participation. This synthesis authorizes no application work and makes no current-year compliance claim.

## 13. Recommended immediate next step

Conduct **one neutral interview with an actual first-time or infrequent martial-arts seminar organizer who personally owned important planning decisions**. Reconstruct one completed or active seminar before any concept disclosure, identify the mentor guidance, prior templates, checklists, documents, chats, and tools actually used, and determine whether a specific consequential gap remained after those alternatives.

It must not include a product pitch, prototype, feature request exercise, or willingness-to-pay claim.
