- **[RI] Researcher inference** - an analyst interpretation that remains unvalidated until checked separately.

2. **Senior coordination.** **[PR]** The two senior organizers held the complete plan and jointly handled important decisions.
3. **Promotion and preparation.** **[PR]** Promotion was part of preparation but repeatedly received less attention and began later than the participant considered preferable.
4. **Delegation.** **[PR]** Other people received only the tasks relevant to their role, mainly through direct verbal instruction and, in some cases, a messaging group.
5. **Explanation and activation.** **[PR]** The participant repeated instructions several times and remained an update point for timing and programme changes.
6. **Late changes and fallback.** **[PR]** Venue availability, helper availability, programme timing, and tatami transport could require replanning; reserve helpers or alternative transport were used when needed.
7. **Event completion.** **[PR]** The described incidents were resolved and did not cancel the event.
8. **Learning for the next event.** **[PR]** The organizers adjusted later plans based on earlier mistakes, including preparing some responsibilities earlier.
