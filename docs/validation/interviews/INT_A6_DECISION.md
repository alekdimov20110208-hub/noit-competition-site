### Decision rationale

- The participant is highly relevant and confirmed knowledge concentration, repeated verbal instruction, and dependence on two senior organizers.
- No real complete handoff or key-organizer absence was described.
- No significant measurable consequence from knowledge-transfer failure was established.
- The strongest recurring issue concerned promotion, not handoff.
- Promotion severity and measurable outcome remain unknown.
- Existing experience, direct communication, reserve people, and iterative improvement generally kept the events operational.
- The participant explicitly described the problems as solvable and mainly improvable.
- The conditions for `PROCEED TO PROBLEM-SPECIFIC PROTOTYPE` are therefore not met.
- `REVISE SEGMENT OR PROBLEM` is not selected because promotion and logistics lack sufficient frequency and consequence evidence for a new narrow problem statement.
- `TARGETED INTERVIEW REQUIRED` is not selected solely from INT-A6 because no single named follow-up with this participant is guaranteed to resolve prevalence or adoption. The already planned independent martial-arts organizer interview is the next allowed comparison step.
- The current product hypothesis is not killed across the whole project. This is a case-level decision about INT-A6 evidence only.

### Next allowed step

Complete the already planned independent interview with another experienced organizer from a different martial art, analyze it separately, and only then compare whether knowledge concentration, repeated explanation, significant consequences, and current-workaround limitations recur independently.

Do not update the global synthesis until that next interview has been analyzed unless an existing repository rule explicitly requires an interim update.
