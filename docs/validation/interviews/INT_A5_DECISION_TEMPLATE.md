## 3. Evidence classification rules

Use the repository’s established distinctions consistently:

- **[PR] Participant report** — the participant’s account of a past or current process, action, incident, or consequence. It supports what this participant reported, not prevalence across a segment.
- **[PO] Participant opinion** — a preference, judgment, interpretation, prediction, recommendation, or future intention stated by the participant.
- **[RI] Researcher inference** — an interpretation derived from cited evidence. It remains an inference until separately tested.
- **[OE] Direct observation / observed evidence** — behavior or a redacted artifact directly observed by the researcher with explicit consent. Record exactly what was observed and the observation boundary.
- **[U] Unknown** — the available evidence does not answer the question or is too unclear to classify.

Rules:

- Do not create a direct-observation claim from a verbal description.
- A shown artifact supports only what was actually visible; it does not prove completeness, effectiveness, authorship, or typical use.
- Keep a near-verbatim statement separate from a paraphrase and an inference.
- Keep contradictory evidence and adequate current alternatives.
- Do not convert one interview into a prevalence claim, percentage, segment validation, or product requirement.
