# INT-A5 — recruitment and screening

- **Date:** 2026-07-21
- **Status:** research preparation only
- **Controlling recommendation:** `TARGETED VALIDATION`
- **Research-plan rule:** `TARGETED INTERVIEW REQUIRED`

This document prepares recruitment and qualification for one neutral INT-A5 interview. It does not identify a participant, record an interview, select Candidate A or Candidate B, validate a product hypothesis, or authorize a prototype or development.

## A. Research objective

The exact decision question is:

> What guidance, mentors, previous templates, checklists, and general tools does an actual first-time or infrequent martial-arts seminar organizer use, and what consequential gap remains after using them?

The interview is intended to examine a real completed or current organizing process. It is **not** intended to validate an application idea, collect feature requests, test willingness to pay, or persuade the participant that another tool is needed. A finding that existing support was adequate is valid and decision-relevant evidence.

## B. Required participant profile

The participant should meet all of the following conditions:

- has personally organized or meaningfully co-organized a martial-arts seminar;
- has completed their first seminar, is actively preparing their first seminar, or organizes seminars too infrequently to have a mature recurring routine;
- had or currently has direct responsibility for important preparation decisions, not only event-day assistance;
- can discuss a real recent or current seminar process in concrete detail;
- is willing to describe the advice, tools, templates, documents, or other guidance actually used.

The participant does **not** need to have experienced a failure. An account in which guidance, templates, and tools worked well is equally relevant.

## C. Exclusion and borderline rules

Normally exclude:

- attendees with no organizing responsibility;
- helpers who only followed instructions and had no planning ownership;
- highly experienced chief organizers with a stable recurring method;
- people discussing only tournaments, regular classes, gradings, demonstrations, or unrelated events;
- people who can provide only hypothetical opinions and cannot reconstruct a real seminar process.

Do not silently force an unclear case into `QUALIFIED` or `NOT QUALIFIED`. Record a borderline case as `BORDERLINE — REVIEW` and preserve:

- the exact organizing role;
- which planning decisions the person owned;
- which decisions were controlled by a more experienced organizer;
- the number and frequency of comparable seminars;
- why the event may or may not qualify as a seminar;
- which criterion remains uncertain;
- who reviewed the qualification and the review result.

An actively preparing first-time organizer may qualify even though the seminar has not yet occurred, provided that real preparation is under way and the participant can discuss actual actions, decisions, guidance, and materials. A co-organizer may qualify when they owned meaningful planning decisions. Optional artifact access and experience of a consequential problem are not qualification requirements.

## D. Screener questions

Use the following concise Bulgarian screener before scheduling the interview. Do not describe a product, proposed feature, guided playbook, or Candidate B hypothesis.

1. **Какво бойно изкуство практикувате или представлявате във връзка със семинара?**
2. **Какъв точно беше или е форматът на събитието?** Моля, уточнете дали е семинар, турнир, изпит, редовна тренировка или друг вид събитие.
3. **Кога се проведе семинарът или на какъв етап е подготовката му в момента?**
4. **Каква беше или е Вашата точна роля в организирането?**
5. **Колко сходни семинара сте организирали или съорганизирали лично и колко често правите това?**
6. **За кои решения и дейности по подготовката отговаряхте лично?** Например може да опишете какво решавахте, координирахте или проверявахте, без да посочвате лични данни.
7. **Имаше ли по-опитен организатор, който контролираше повечето важни решения?** Ако да, кои решения оставаха реално Ваши?
8. **Можете ли да обсъдите конкретния реален процес — как започна, какво направихте и какви съвети, документи или инструменти използвахте?**
9. **Ако по време на разговора стане полезно, бихте ли обсъдили по желание празен, заличен или обобщен пример на използван материал?** Това не е задължително и ще се прави само след отделно съгласие; не изпращайте материала предварително.

Record the answers as short factual notes. Do not infer responsibilities that the person did not describe.

## E. Recruitment message

> Здравейте! Провеждам кратко изследователско интервю за реалния опит при организиране на семинари по бойни изкуства — как протича подготовката, откъде идват насоките и какви материали и инструменти се използват на практика. Търся човек, който е организирал или съорганизирал първия си семинар, подготвя такъв в момента или го прави рядко. Разговорът е около 30–45 минути, не изисква предварителна подготовка и не е търговска презентация. Не са нужни имена на участници или други лични данни; в изследователската документация се използва код на интервюто и анонимизирани бележки. Ако профилът Ви отговаря и желаете да участвате доброволно, ще изпратя няколко кратки въпроса за уточнение.

The invitation may be sent by direct message, email, or chat. Do not add claims about a product or promise absolute anonymity beyond the repository process described above.

## F. Qualification decision

### `QUALIFIED`

Required evidence:

- the event is a real martial-arts seminar that has been completed or is actively being prepared;
- the participant personally organized or meaningfully co-organized it;
- the participant owned important preparation decisions or planning work;
- the participant is a first-time organizer or organizes too infrequently to have a mature recurring routine;
- the participant can reconstruct actual actions and discuss guidance, tools, templates, or documents used.

Record one short evidence statement for every criterion. Do not use willingness to criticize the process, willingness to show artifacts, or experience of a failure as a substitute criterion.

### `BORDERLINE — REVIEW`

Use when most criteria appear plausible but at least one material boundary is unclear, for example:

- meaningful co-organization is claimed, but an experienced organizer controlled most decisions;
- the event combined a seminar with a tournament, grading, or regular class and the seminar process is not yet separable;
- the participant has organized several seminars, but it is unclear whether a mature routine exists;
- planning responsibility or the reality/current status of the process needs confirmation.

Required record:

- evidence supporting qualification;
- evidence against qualification;
- the unresolved criterion;
- reviewer name or placeholder, review date, and final decision.

Do not schedule the full interview until the borderline review is resolved or the research owner explicitly accepts the case as a documented borderline participant.

### `NOT QUALIFIED`

Use when clear evidence shows any decisive mismatch, including:

- no martial-arts seminar was involved;
- the person was only an attendee or instruction-following helper;
- no meaningful planning responsibility existed;
- the person is a highly experienced chief organizer with a stable recurring method;
- only hypothetical opinions can be provided.

Record the decisive exclusion reason without collecting unnecessary personal details.

## G. Privacy and artifact boundary

- Do not copy a private participant list, registration export, payment data, phone numbers, addresses, travel documents, or sensitive instructor information.
- Artifacts are optional and are not required for qualification or interview completion.
- Ask for separate, explicit consent before recording or reviewing any artifact, chat, document, spreadsheet, calendar, or image.
- Prefer a blank, redacted, or researcher-described example. Do not ask the participant to send an original private file when a verbal description is sufficient.
- Only redacted summaries or researcher notes may enter the repository. Do not commit screenshots, private messages, consent forms, identifying source material, or unredacted artifacts.
- Raw audio recordings and raw transcripts must remain outside Git. Record only their approved storage location outside Git, not their contents or an identifying public link.
- Use an interview code rather than a name or initials. Do not record unnecessary identifying dates, places, organization names, account names, photographs, or contact details.
- If consent is refused for recording, continue with consented written notes if the participant agrees. If consent is refused for written notes, do not create an interview record.
- Honor any request to skip a question, stop the interview, exclude an artifact, or remove a portion from the research notes.
