# Interview INT-SATO Notes

## 1. Evidence status and privacy boundary

This is an empty interview-notes template. Complete it only from a consented interview and authorized source material.

Privacy and source rules:

- Raw audio and transcripts stay outside Git. Do not record a private source path in this file.
- Do not include personal contact details or unnecessary personal information.
- Anonymize sensitive or identifying details before they enter this file.
- Automatic transcription may contain errors.
- Any quotation requires verification against authorized audio before use.
- Record written-notes consent and audio-recording consent explicitly and separately.
- If written-notes consent is not given, do not create an interview evidence record.
- If audio-recording consent is refused, continue only with permitted written notes.
- Keep behavioral evidence, participant opinion, researcher inference, and contaminated concept feedback separate.
- Do not treat concept feedback as behavioral problem, adoption, demand, or willingness-to-pay evidence.

Evidence labels:

- **[PR] Participant report** - account of a past or current process, action, incident, or consequence.
- **[PO] Participant opinion** - judgment, preference, prediction, recommendation, or future intention.
- **[CF] Contaminated concept feedback** - response after the application concept was explained or after a leading product prompt.
- **[RI] Researcher inference** - analyst interpretation that remains unvalidated until checked separately.
- **[U] Unknown** - the evidence does not answer the question or is too unreliable to classify.
