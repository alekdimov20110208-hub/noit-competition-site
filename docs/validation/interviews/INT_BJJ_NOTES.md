# Interview INT-BJJ Notes

## 1. Evidence status and privacy boundary

This file records one completed, anonymized validation interview with an experienced Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu instructor and event organizer. It contains careful paraphrases from the approved evidence summary derived from an automatically generated transcript with recognition errors. It does not contain the participant's name, contact details, organization or club name, exact event titles, exact dates, exact locations, a raw or lightly cleaned transcript, or a private path to source material.

The raw audio and transcript remain outside Git and are not referenced here. No quotation, uncertain proper name, exact amount, or exact date is treated as verified. The approved evidence summary contains no direct observation of an organizational artifact, live workflow, prototype use, purchase, or payment behavior.

Evidence labels used in this record:

- **[PR] Participant report** - an account of a past or current process, action, incident, or consequence.
- **[PO] Participant opinion** - a judgment, preference, prediction, recommendation, or future intention.
- **[CF] Contaminated concept feedback** - a response given after the interviewer explained a possible product or used a leading prompt.
- **[RI] Researcher inference** - an analyst interpretation that remains a hypothesis until independently tested.
- **[U] Unknown** - the evidence does not answer the question or the transcript quality is insufficient.

- **[RI]** The evidence for centralization is strong as a current process condition but only moderate as a consequential problem because no organizer-absence failure was established.
- **[RI]** The current workaround is viable at the participant's present scale, so a replacement for Viber or calls is not justified by this interview.
- **[RI]** A lightweight coordination layer that coexists with existing communication is a testable direction, not a validated solution.
- **[RI]** Event scale and team experience may moderate problem severity; this must be tested with independent organizers.
- **[RI]** Late registration and payment may require a different product response from task and contingency coordination. This interview does not justify combining them into one product scope.
